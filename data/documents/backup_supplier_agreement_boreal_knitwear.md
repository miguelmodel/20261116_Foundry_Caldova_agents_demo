# Caldova | Backup Supplier Agreement - Boreal Knitwear

Fictitious demo data. Boreal Knitwear, its capacity and all commercial terms below are invented for demonstration. Effective date: 2026-11-03. Currency: EUR.

## Approved scope

Boreal Knitwear operates in Porto, Portugal. It is an approved contingency supplier for the Linen Relaxed Shirt and Satin Midi Skirt; the company name does not restrict this fictional approved product scope. Quality and color matching have been pre-approved for the demo SKUs only. No alternative is approved for the denim or cotton-tee SKU in this snapshot.

The goods premium is 18% over the reference price. Capacities are per SKU, independently available, and represent a one-time contingency allocation rather than a recurring daily supply. The minimum accepted contingency order is 500 units.

## Lead time and routing

An order accepted on 2026-11-03 reaches the nominated EU DC after six calendar days, on 2026-11-09. This includes production finishing, quality release and road transport from Porto. Do not add a second six-day transit leg. Store delivery takes one additional day, giving store availability on 2026-11-10.

Supplier-to-DC road freight is EUR 0.65 per unit. Additional DC-to-store delivery is also EUR 0.65 per unit. All route estimates are fictitious planning values and require confirmation before purchase.

## Financial treatment

A 5,000-unit linen order costs EUR 91,450 for goods, EUR 3,250 inbound road and EUR 3,250 final mile: EUR 97,950 of additional cash commitment. Its goods-only premium over Atlas pricing is EUR 13,950. The premium alone is not the approval amount.

A 2,500-unit satin order costs EUR 53,100 for goods plus EUR 3,250 total road freight: EUR 56,350. The goods-only premium is EUR 8,100. There is no assumption of a markdown, resale or cancellation credit for later excess stock.

## Conditions and limits

- The original PO remains payable; a backup purchase creates additional inventory when the original shipment eventually arrives.
- Procurement must reconfirm the quantity and six-day commitment at acceptance. The ERP snapshot is not a live reservation.
- Order only approved SKUs, in quantities from 500 units up to the listed cap.
- Human purchase approval follows the total incident commitment, including any associated stock transfers.
- The supplier may not substitute fabric or color without a separate human quality approval.

## Decision guidance

Backup production avoids emergency air transport but arrives later than the four-day origin-to-DC air option. It cannot retroactively repair a stockout before 2026-11-10. Existing DC and donor-store stock can bridge that gap, but donor exposure must be disclosed.

Prefer a transparent comparison of availability, full new cash, remaining inventory and approval level. Do not describe the supplier as unlimited or assume it can replace all 12,000 delayed Atlas units.
