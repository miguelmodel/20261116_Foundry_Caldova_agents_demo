# Caldova | Supplier Agreement - Atlas Textiles Ltd

Fictitious demo data. This is an invented commercial agreement for demonstration, not a legal contract. Effective date: 2026-11-03. All amounts are EUR.

## Parties and covered purchase

Buyer: Caldova Demo Retail EU. Supplier: Atlas Textiles Ltd, Dhaka, Bangladesh. Product: CV-LIN-0042, Linen Relaxed Shirt. Shipment: SH-2041. Quantity: 12,000 units. Contracted unit price: EUR 15.50. Purchase order value: EUR 186,000, excluding freight and tax.

The agreed destination is DC Guadalajara via Chittagong and Valencia. Original delivery date is 2026-11-10. The revised DC delivery date is 2026-11-19, nine calendar days late. Final-mile store delivery is a separate one-day planning leg.

## Late-delivery credit

- A grace period of three calendar days runs after the original DC delivery date.
- Thereafter, each late calendar day attracts 1.5% of the full covered PO value.
- The total credit is capped at 15% of PO value. Days are not rounded upward from partial days in this demo.
- Formula: PO value x min(0.15, max(0, delay days - 3) x 0.015).
- At nine days late: six chargeable days x 1.5% x EUR 186,000 = EUR 16,740. The cap is EUR 27,900.

The credit is a projected claim, subject to receipt evidence and Procurement acceptance. It is not cash already received. Do not deduct it from the recovery plan's approval amount.

For this specific demo PO, an approved partial air diversion does not change the full-PO claim basis or the delay of the undiverted balance. Do not extrapolate that convention to other suppliers. No late-delivery credit is defined in this snapshot for Jade Weave or Harbor Denim.

## Notification duty

Atlas must notify Procurement within 48 hours after becoming aware that the delivery commitment is at risk. The notice must state the cause, affected quantity, expected delay and proposed mitigation. Update the notice within 24 hours of a material change.

For SH-2041, Atlas recorded awareness on 2026-10-25 at 09:00 UTC and notified Caldova on 2026-10-26 at 09:00 UTC. The 48-hour notification requirement was met. Notification alone does not waive the delivery credit.

## Force majeure

A supplier may request a waiver for an extraordinary event outside its reasonable control, supported by evidence of unavoidable impact and mitigation. Procurement must explicitly accept the waiver in writing before the credit is treated as waived.

Ordinary utility interruptions, routine factory capacity problems and foreseeable port congestion are not automatically accepted as force majeure. SH-2041 cites six days of factory power outages and three days of congestion. Its waiver status is "not approved"; the scenario therefore shows the formula-based claim as potentially recoverable.

If a waiver is later approved, eligible recovery becomes zero. If delay changes, recalculate the grace period and cap. The Analyst must disclose these assumptions instead of giving legal advice.

## Recovery cooperation

Atlas has quality-released the covered goods and made them accessible in bonded origin holding on 2026-11-03. Up to 3,000 units can be diverted to air before sea loading that day, subject to carrier confirmation. The remaining units continue by sea. Additional Boreal purchases do not cancel the original Atlas PO.

Procurement maintains notices, receipts, waiver decisions and the final claim. Logistics retains the diversion confirmation and revised packing list.
